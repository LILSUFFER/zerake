## Your weak spot

The only serious threat is **phishing**. If a scammer gets access to your ClubGG account, they cannot withdraw your chips directly, but they can lose them to another player at the table.

**How to protect yourself:**
- never share your password or verification codes, even with "admins" — we will never ask for them;
- log in only through the official ClubGG app and do not follow links from private messages;
- the only club website is **zerake.com** — there are no other sites or mirrors;
- if you notice suspicious activity, contact us immediately.
